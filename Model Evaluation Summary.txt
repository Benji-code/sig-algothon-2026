- Exposure:
	- Same as SigmaB10: ALGO hedge off, plain 5-day reversal, full $100k cap
- Performance:
	- Days 251-500: 822
	- Days 501-750: 825
	- Days 751-1000:
*Best-by-backtest model in the lineage as of this entry, beating everything on 251-500 outright and everything but Sigma17 on 501-750 (discounted, since Sigma17's number came paired with a worse 251-500 and is the worst live performer in the lineage). Sourced from a received ChatGPT-authored model (Sigma7p_robust_fit500.py in Model List), which bundled 7 changes together; ablated to isolate this one piece as responsible for ~69% of that file's out-of-sample improvement, neighbourhood-tested clean across threshold/weight/blend (19 configs, zero exceptions), then grafted onto SigmaB10's chassis and reconfirmed clean on both windows. Not yet tested live. See Alpha Exploration Log entry 11 for the full validation chain.
