HOW TO INTERPRET THE AUDIT
No individual failure proves that the strategy is overfit, and no collection of passes proves the historical score will repeat. The strongest warning combination is: high concentration, weak leave-block-out results, isolated parameter peaks, positive placebo scores, and rapid collapse as relationship retention falls.
This audit does not remove the selection bias created by using the same 1,000 days to develop many strategy generations.
